IGC Pharma, Inc. · NYSE American: IGC · Potomac, Maryland

IGC Pharma says it is a clinical-stage biotechnology company, with a primary emphasis on Alzheimer’s disease.

In the company’s own words, IGC Pharma “is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on the development of novel therapeutic candidates for neuropsychiatric and neurodegenerative disorders, with a primary emphasis on Alzheimer’s disease.” Its lead candidate, IGC-AD1, is in CALMA, a Phase 2 trial for agitation in Alzheimer’s dementia. Company statement 10-KT

Read first: four things the filings say

At June 30, 2026 the company had cash and cash equivalents of $331,000 and, in its words, “a working capital deficit of approximately $2.0 million.” Filed 10-Q The company estimates that its cash, investments, anticipated financing and amounts reasonably expected to be available under its $12 million O-Bank facility will support operations for at least twelve months following the issuance of the statements (the 10-Q was filed Aug 14, 2026), “although there can be no assurance.” The facility expires May 31, 2027, borrowings under it are limited to $1 million a month, and the estimate does not assume renewal. The trial

CALMA: six weeks of treatment, and what the filings say so far

The company describes agitation in Alzheimer’s dementia as “associated with accelerated disease progression, increased institutionalization, and significant caregiver burden.” CALMA tests IGC-AD1 against placebo for that symptom. Company statement 10-KT

The design

  • A multi-site, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 2 study at clinical sites in the United States and Canada.
  • IGC-AD1 or placebo, taken twice daily.
  • Primary endpoint: change from baseline in agitation, measured with validated components of the Cohen-Mansfield Agitation Inventory (CMAI), over a six-week treatment period.
  • Secondary endpoint: evaluated over a two-week treatment period.
  • Exploratory: other neuropsychiatric domains, caregiver distress, sleep disturbance and blood-based biomarkers.
  • 146 patients completing the trial.

Filed 10-KT

Results and safety, as stated

The 10-KT says the Phase 1 study showed IGC-AD1 “was generally well tolerated across evaluated dose levels, with no treatment-related serious adverse events reported.” It also says interim analyses on the first 26 CALMA patients “indicated that patients receiving IGC-AD1 experienced reductions in agitation compared to placebo,” and that “These interim findings are preliminary and subject to further analysis upon completion of the trial.” Elsewhere the 10-KT says interim data “have demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in agitation symptoms compared to placebo” and that the analysis on the first 26 patients “indicated a large effect size (Cohen’s d = 0.79)”; the 10-Q says interim and blinded observations “are preliminary, are not statistically powered to establish efficacy, and may not be predictive of topline or final results.” Company statement 10-KT 10-Q

The September 28 release says the Data Safety Monitoring Committee “has recommended that the trial continue without modification” at each review to date, that, in its words, “As of the Company’s most recent safety reporting, blinded safety monitoring had not identified any treatment-related serious adverse events as related to study treatment” (the release does not date that reporting), and that safety data remain subject to final review after database lock and unblinding. Company site Sep 28 release

The treatment setting

The 10-KT notes that in May 2023 the FDA approved brexpiprazole, “an atypical antipsychotic, with a boxed warning,” as the first medication for agitation associated with Alzheimer’s disease, following “a significantly larger 12-week Phase 3 trial.” CALMA is a six-week Phase 2 study. IGC-AD1 “has not been approved for commercial use” by the FDA “or any other regulatory authority.” Company statement 10-KT

AI platform

MINT-AD: an AI decision-support tool in development

The 10-KT describes MINT-AD as “a proprietary, artificial intelligence, enabled data platform designed to support risk stratification and longitudinal assessment in Alzheimer’s disease using multimodal datasets,” and says it “is not currently approved as a diagnostic device.” Company statement 10-KT

The 10-KT says the platform is designed to support the clinical workflow through three primary objectives. Company statement 10-KT

  1. Risk stratification. “Utilizing AI to analyze multimodal data and identify individuals at high risk of Alzheimer’s disease.”
  2. Predictive modeling. “Forecasting cognitive decline trajectories two to five years in advance.”
  3. Structured plan support. “Assisting the clinician in creating a structured intervention plan for the individual.”

Behind it, the company describes AHA as a research-layer framework that “is not intended for clinical use,” and says AHA “was selected as a semi-finalist” in the Alzheimer’s Disease Data Initiative USD $1 million Prize competition in 2025. Company statement 10-KT

On October 13, 2026 the company announced PRISM as “a new application layer” for MINT-AD and said it is “evaluating” business-to-business, business-to-business-to-consumer and online commercialization opportunities. Company site Oct 13 release
